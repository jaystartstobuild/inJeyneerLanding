The 4-Stage Technical Resume Calibration

A battle-tested prompt pipeline to audit, score, rewrite, and stress-test technical resumes against an exact job description without hallucinations or AI clichés.

Harness Specifications v2.0 Production
  • 01 Gap & Claim Matrix (Diagnoser)
  • 02 Calibrated 100-Pt Rubric (Recruiter)
  • 03 Google XYZ Rewriter (Anti-Hallucination)
  • 04 Gap-Targeted Interview Pressure Test
Harness Pipeline

4 Steps.
Zero Hallucinations.

  1. 00. Prepare Fact Bank
  2. 01. Run Gap Diagnoser
  3. 02. Run Recruiter Scorer
  4. 03. Run XYZ Rewriter
  5. 04. Run Interview Test
Engineering Rule: Never run all passes in one vague prompt. Context degradation leads to hallucinated metrics. Run each pass sequentially.

Most AI resume prompts fail for a simple reason: asking an LLM to "make my resume better" triggers generic corporate buzzwords, fabricated metrics, and hallucinated tools. When a technical recruiter scans the output, it screams "generated by ChatGPT" and lands in the rejection pile.

This guide provides a structured four-pass prompt harness. By isolating diagnosis, scoring, rewriting, and interview questioning into separate steps with strict guardrails, you get high-signal, defensible resume bullets grounded in verifiable truth.

Setup: Build Your Fact Bank

Before prompting the model, you need three clean inputs. The most critical is the Fact Bank: a raw scratchpad of real metrics, technologies, architectures, and numbers from your career.

PREP Fact Bank Template (Fill this out first) 
### CANDIDATE FACT BANK (VERIFIED ONLY)

- Systems & Services: [e.g. Auth microservice, Payment worker, ETL data pipeline]
- Scale & Traffic: [e.g. 5,000 req/sec, 1.2M daily active users, 4TB daily ingestion]
- Performance Metrics: [e.g. reduced p99 latency from 450ms to 85ms, cut compute cost 32%]
- Team & Scope: [e.g. led 4 engineers, collaborated with 2 product managers, cross-functional with SecOps]
- Tooling & Stack: [e.g. Go, Python, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, AWS ECS, Terraform, Kubernetes]
- Key Deliverables: [e.g. migrated monolith to event-driven queue; rebuilt indexing engine]

Pass 1: The Gap & Claim Auditor (Diagnoser)

Objective: Compare your current resume line-by-line against the target job description. The model acts as a forensic resume auditor, producing an audit table that exposes unquantified claims, vague buzzwords, and missing prerequisites.

PASS 01 Diagnoser · Gap & Claim Auditor 
You are a Senior Technical Recruiter and Staff Engineering Hiring Auditor. 
Your job is to conduct a ruthless line-by-line comparison between my raw resume and the target job description. Do not rewrite anything yet. Do not infer or assume experience that is not explicitly stated.

### INPUTS:

[PASTE FULL JOB DESCRIPTION HERE]



[PASTE YOUR CURRENT RESUME HERE]


### INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Parse the target job description into required qualifications, preferred qualifications, and core domain responsibilities.
2. Produce a Markdown comparison table with the following columns:
   | Job Requirement | Resume Evidence (Quote exact line or "NONE") | Status (STRONG / PARTIAL / MISSING) | Vulnerability & Action Needed |
3. List all Vague Phrases in my resume (e.g. "helped with", "responsible for", "participated in", "worked on") that lack clear ownership or measurable metrics.
4. Highlight any Unsubstantiated Claims where I claimed an outcome (e.g. "improved performance") without stating baseline, metric, or technical mechanism.
5. Provide a summary of the 3 biggest disqualifying gaps that would prevent me from passing an initial screening.

Pass 2: The Calibrated Recruiter Rubric (Scorer)

Objective: Score the resume out of 100 points against the target job description using a strict screening rubric. The model must justify every point deduction with an exact requirement from the job post.

PASS 02 Recruiter · Calibrated Rubric Scorer 
You are a Technical Talent Partner evaluating candidates for this exact role. Grade my resume against the job description using the calibrated 100-point rubric below. 

Be objective, strict, and evidence-based. For every deduction, cite the exact missing or weak requirement.

### 100-POINT SCORING RUBRIC:
1. Core Technical Stack & Language Match (Max 40 points):
   - Direct production experience with primary languages/frameworks requested.
2. Scope, Scale & Seniority Match (Max 30 points):
   - Evidence of handling equivalent systems scale, architectural autonomy, or team leadership.
3. Quantified Engineering & Business Impact (Max 20 points):
   - Clear XYZ format bullets with measurable baselines (latency, throughput, revenue, efficiency).
4. Clarity, Density & 6-Second Scan Signal (Max 10 points):
   - Information density, clear timeline, no fluff, easy for an engineer to skim in 6 seconds.

### REQUIRED OUTPUT:
1. Total Score: [Score] / 100
2. Category Breakdown: Show points awarded and point deductions with exact explanations.
3. The 6-Second First Impression: What an engineer or recruiter notices in the first 6 seconds.
4. The Disqualification Risk: The #1 reason this resume would get rejected in round one.
5. Top 3 Highest-ROI Fixes: The three specific changes that would yield the biggest score increase.

Pass 3: The Zero-Hallucination XYZ Rewriter

Objective: Rewrite supported bullets using Google's "Accomplished [X], measured by [Y], by doing [Z]" formula. The model is strictly forbidden from inventing metrics or technologies; if data is missing, it must insert a metric placeholder.

× Banned AI Clichés (Strictly Forbidden in Output)
spearheaded leveraged revolutionized pivotal passionate testament synergy orchestrating dynamic robust
× Weak / AI-Generated Bullet
"Spearheaded database optimization efforts using cutting-edge techniques to drastically improve system performance and boost user satisfaction."
✓ Grounded XYZ Engineering Bullet
"Reduced PostgreSQL p99 query latency from 320ms to 45ms by partitioning high-churn event tables and implementing Redis read-through caching."
PASS 03 Rewriter · Zero-Hallucination XYZ Engine 
You are an elite Staff Engineer and Technical Resume Editor. 
Your task is to rewrite my resume bullets using the Google XYZ Formula:
"Accomplished [X: Business / Technical Outcome], as measured by [Y: Concrete Metric], by doing [Z: Technical Action, Architecture, or Tooling]."

### INPUTS:

[PASTE YOUR FACT BANK SCRATCHPAD HERE]


### CRITICAL ANTI-HALLUCINATION RULES:
1. ONLY use facts, tools, and metrics explicitly provided in my resume or fact bank.
2. If a metric, team size, or performance number is missing, DO NOT invent one. Instead, insert: [INSERT METRIC: e.g. latency in ms, QPS, or % decrease].
3. STRICTLY FORBIDDEN WORDS: Do not use generic AI buzzwords: "spearheaded", "leveraged", "pivotal", "revolutionized", "synergy", "testament", "orchestrating", "dynamic", "robust", "delved".
4. Use active, precise engineering verbs: e.g., "Architected", "Refactored", "Profiled", "Automated", "Benchmarked", "Provisioned", "Migrated".

### REQUIRED OUTPUT FORMAT FOR EACH REWRITTEN BULLET:
- [BEFORE]: Original resume bullet
- [AFTER (XYZ)]: Accomplished [X], measured by [Y], by doing [Z]
- [EVIDENCE SOURCE]: What part of the fact bank/resume verified this claim, or [NEEDS FACT] if verification is needed.

Pass 4: The Technical Interview Pressure-Test

Objective: Put your rewritten resume to the test. The model roleplays as the Engineering Manager conducting the hiring loop, formulating deep technical questions that specifically target remaining gaps, architectural choices, and claimed metrics.

PASS 04 Hiring Manager · Gap Pressure-Test 
You are the Hiring Engineering Manager interviewing me for this exact role. You have scrutinized my audited resume and the target job description.

Generate 6 to 8 deep, technical interview questions based strictly on:
1. The largest gaps between the role requirements and my experience.
2. The most aggressive technical claims or metrics on my resume that demand proof.
3. System design or architectural tradeoffs implied by my bullet points.

### FOR EACH QUESTION, PROVIDE:
1. The Question: An authentic, challenging technical interview question.
2. Hidden Intent: What the interviewer is actually probing (e.g. "Testing if they actually owned the architecture or just maintained it").
3. The Trap: The classic blundering or defensive answer that gets candidates rejected.
4. High-Signal Honest Framework: A structured 3-part framework on how to answer honestly—even if I have a qualification gap—without defensive hedging or bluffing.

All-In-One Master Prompt (For Claude Projects / Advanced Chats)

If you prefer to run the entire four-pass review in a single interactive session (such as a Claude Project or long ChatGPT thread), use this master orchestrator prompt with structured XML input delimiters:

ALL-IN-ONE Master Chained Pipeline Harness 
You are an elite Staff Engineer and Technical Hiring Screener. Execute a sequential Four-Pass Review of my resume against the target role. 

CRITICAL GUARDRAILS:
- Do not invent experience or metrics.
- Ban all AI clichés: "spearheaded", "leveraged", "pivotal", "revolutionized", "synergy", "testament", "robust".
- Mark missing metrics with [INSERT METRIC].

### INPUT DATA:

[PASTE TARGET JOB DESCRIPTION HERE]



[PASTE CURRENT RESUME HERE]



[PASTE FACT BANK WITH VERIFIED METRICS HERE]


### EXECUTE IN 4 SEQUENTIAL SECTIONS:

### SECTION 1: GAP & CLAIM AUDITOR (DIAGNOSER)
Produce a Markdown audit table comparing every stated job requirement against my resume evidence, rating each as STRONG, PARTIAL, or MISSING. List all vague bullets lacking clear ownership.

### SECTION 2: CALIBRATED RECRUITER SCORING (100 PTS)
Score out of 100 based on Tech Stack Match (40 pts), Scope & Scale (30 pts), Quantified Impact (20 pts), and 6-Second Scan Signal (10 pts). Itemize every point deduction.

### SECTION 3: ZERO-HALLUCINATION XYZ REWRITER
Rewrite every supported bullet point using: "Accomplished [X], measured by [Y], by doing [Z]". Use only facts from my resume and fact bank. Show BEFORE and AFTER for each.

### SECTION 4: HIRING MANAGER PRESSURE-TEST
Provide 6 technical interview questions targeting my biggest gaps, along with the interviewer's hidden intent, the common trap, and an honest high-signal answer framework.

Final Audit Verification

Before exporting your resume to PDF, run this final pre-flight checklist:

  • Metric Traceability: Can you explain how every percentage, latency reduction, and dollar figure was calculated in an interview?
  • No Banned AI Words: Search your draft for words like spearheaded or leveraged and eliminate them.
  • Defensible Gaps: Ensure any unmet requirements have an interview strategy prepared (from Pass 4) rather than fake bullet points.
